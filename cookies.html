🍪 Cookies & digital seeds

Just like we nurture plants, we care about your privacy. This Cookies Policy explains how Ozone Flora Hub uses cookies, similar technologies, and how you can manage your preferences while browsing our red-themed garden universe.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help us remember your preferences, understand how you interact with our content, and improve your gardening experience. Think of them as tiny digital seeds that make our site bloom just for you.

How we use cookies

At Ozone Flora Hub, we use cookies to enhance your journey through red-themed landscapes, plant guides, and workshop bookings. The categories we deploy include:

  • Essential cookies: Necessary for website functionality and security (e.g., navigation, form submissions).
  • Performance & analytics: To count visits and traffic sources, so we can measure and improve our garden content.
  • Functional cookies: Remember your preferences (language, region, saved favorite red perennials).
  • Targeting cookies: Help deliver relevant gardening ads and workshop suggestions (only with your consent).

Third-party services (like embedded YouTube videos or map widgets) may also set cookies. We do not control those, but we recommend reviewing their policies.

Your consent & choices

When you first visit Ozone Flora Hub, you'll see a cookie banner where you can accept or customize non-essential cookies. You can change your mind anytime via the floating button below (opens preference panel).

Manage your cookie settings

You can withdraw consent or adjust cookie categories (Analytics, Functional, Targeting) at any moment. Essential cookies will always remain active to keep the site running smoothly.

You can also configure cookies via your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect some interactive features like workshop reminders or personalized garden recommendations.

Ozone Flora Hub may occasionally refresh this Cookies Policy to reflect new technologies or legal standards. We’ll always display the “effective date” at the bottom. Your continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy. In case of significant modifications, we will notify you with a notice on our website.
